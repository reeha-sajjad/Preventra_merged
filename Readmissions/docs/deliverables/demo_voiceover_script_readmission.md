# Preventra Readmission Risk — demo voiceover script

About 3½–4 minutes at a natural pace (~550 words). Each scene has an **On screen** cue for
whoever records the video, and the **Narration** to feed to ElevenLabs. A clean,
narration-only version for pasting is at the bottom.

Recording tips:
- Sign in as a **case manager** (or superadmin) so Overview, Patients, the alerts bell and
  the Clinician Console are all visible.
- Pick one patient with a rising trend and a red flag before recording, and use it in
  scenes 4–6.
- Patient IDs are never read aloud; the narration refers to "this patient".

---

## Scene 1 — Sign in (≈20 s)

**On screen:** The Preventra portal. Type email and password, sign in. The "Choose an
application" screen appears; click **Readmission Risk**.

**Narration:**
Every year, millions of patients are readmitted to hospital within thirty days of going
home. Many of those readmissions are preventable, if the care team knows who is at risk,
and why, in time to act.

This is Preventra. One secure sign-in gives each person access to the products their role
allows. Here, we open Readmission Risk.

## Scene 2 — Overview dashboard (≈30 s)

**On screen:** The Overview page: risk bands, insurer mix, needs-attention counts. Hover
the risk band chart.

**Narration:**
The overview shows the hospital's whole discharged population at a glance: how many
patients sit in the high, medium and low risk bands right now, who pays for their care,
and how many need attention this week.

Access is role-based from the first click. Doctors and nurses see the patients assigned to
them. Case managers see their hospital. Insurers see their own members.

## Scene 3 — Patient worklist (≈35 s)

**On screen:** Patients page. Click **Needs Attention**, then pick a condition (e.g. Heart
failure) in the condition filter. Show the trend arrows and the care team column. Briefly
show **Export CSV**.

**Narration:**
The patient worklist is where the day starts. Every discharged patient is ranked by their
current risk of readmission, with how much that risk has changed since discharge.

One click narrows the list to the patients who need attention, those whose risk is rising
or who need action now. You can filter by condition, export the list, and assign a doctor
and nurses to each patient, so every high-risk patient has an owner.

## Scene 4 — Patient risk score and drivers (≈40 s)

**On screen:** Open a patient. Show the current risk score and band, then scroll to
**Primary Drivers**, **Score History** and **Suggested Clinical Actions**.

**Narration:**
Opening a patient shows their readmission risk. At discharge it is scored by a calibrated
machine learning model, so a score of sixty percent means roughly six in ten similar
patients were readmitted.

But a number alone is not enough to act on. Preventra shows the primary drivers behind
each score, each explained in plain language: a short gap since the last admission, a
history of repeat admissions, an abnormal lab result before discharge. And it suggests
clinical actions the team can take.

## Scene 5 — Weekly post-discharge monitoring (≈50 s)

**On screen:** Open the full weekly trend. Show the chart, the "Monitored as" card, the
recommended action banner, then a week card with a red flag, its drivers and explanations,
and the data sources. Click **Explain this week**.

**Narration:**
Risk does not stop changing when the patient leaves. Preventra re-scores every patient
every seven days across the thirty-day window after discharge.

Each week combines the signals that matter for that patient's condition: weight from a
home device, medication adherence from the patient's app, refills from the pharmacy, and
visits to the clinic. A heart failure patient is watched for fluid gain; a stroke
patient for blood pressure and blood thinners. Every reading shows where it came from.

When something needs a clinician today, a red flag appears at the top of the week. And
with one click, Preventra explains the week in plain language: what changed, why it
matters, and what to do next.

## Scene 6 — Alerts and the clinician console (≈25 s)

**On screen:** Click the alerts bell ("Your patients' risk went up"). Then open the
**Clinician Console** and respond to an alert.

**Narration:**
When a patient's risk rises, the right people hear about it. The alerts bell tells each
doctor and nurse about their own patients, and the clinician console routes each alert to
the responsible clinician, who records their response, so nothing is missed and every
decision is on record.

## Scene 7 — The AI assistant (≈45 s)

**On screen:** Open the chat. Show the common questions, click **Who are the 5
highest-risk patients right now?** Click a patient ID in the answer to open that patient.
Expand the chat to full screen, ask **What are the most common conditions among high-risk
patients?**, and click **Heart failure** in the answer to filter the worklist.

**Narration:**
Finally, the Preventra assistant lets anyone on the team ask questions in plain English.

Start from a common question, or type your own. Every answer is built from your hospital's
live data, and only from the patients you are allowed to see.

Answers are interactive. Click a patient to open their record. Click a condition, and the
worklist filters to those patients instantly. For longer sessions, the assistant expands
to full screen.

## Scene 8 — Close (≈20 s)

**On screen:** Back to the Overview, or the Analytics page showing model performance.

**Narration:**
From sign-in to the patient who needs a call today, Preventra turns discharge data into
action: who is at risk, why, how that is changing week by week, and what to do about it.

Preventra. Fewer readmissions, better recoveries.
