## Voiceover only — for TTS / narrator

> Paste this block and nothing else. Write "GLP-1" as **G L P one** in the TTS input or it
> will be read as "glip-one". Em dashes are intended pauses. Do not include scene headings.

Jen is fifty-three. Today she starts G L P one therapy. On paper, everything is in place — a prescription, a plan, and a follow-up in twelve weeks.

But therapy isn't won or lost in the clinic. It's won or lost in the ten weeks nobody is watching.

A copay higher than she expected. A side effect she doesn't want to make a fuss about. A refill she keeps meaning to pick up.

None of it reaches her care team. Not yet.

Preventra reads the signals already in her utilization data — and helps identify patients who may be at elevated risk of discontinuing therapy. Weeks before the next appointment.

It doesn't stop at the flag. It surfaces the likely drivers behind it — cost pressure, a disrupted appointment, friction with side effects — so the team knows what to address, not just who to call.

So the conversation happens now, while there's still time to act. The decision stays where it belongs — with Jen's care team.

And the same intelligence scales. Across a population, Preventra gives providers and payers visibility into adherence patterns, intervention priority, and downstream cost exposure — so attention reaches the patients who need it first.

Preventra G L P one Intelligence. From G L P one utilization to treatment intelligence.

## Claim compliance

Every claim in this script is hedged to the approved language. If a line is reworded for pacing,
these constraints survive the rewrite:

| Must not say | Used instead |
|---|---|
| Guarantees patients stay on therapy | "may be at elevated risk of discontinuing" |
| Guarantees improved outcomes | "helps identify", "surfaces the likely drivers" |
| Preventra decides treatment | "The decision stays where it belongs — with Jen's care team" |
| Guarantees cost savings / ROI | "gives providers and payers visibility into … downstream cost exposure" |

Scene 7's second sentence is the decision-support disclaimer. It is load-bearing — do not cut it
for time.
