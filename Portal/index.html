Two products, one sign-in

Each one watches a moment when patients are easy to lose. Your account opens whichever your hospital uses.

GLP-1 Analytics

Keep patients on their GLP-1 treatment

For patients on GLP-1 medicines for diabetes or weight, where many stop within months.

  • Each patient’s chance of stopping, and why
  • Groups of patients with similar reasons, to plan support
  • What stopping costs, for the people who hold the budget
Readmission Risk

Stop avoidable returns to hospital

For the weeks after discharge, when a patient who gets worse can be caught early.

  • A risk score for each patient at discharge
  • Weekly re-scoring as monitoring data comes in
  • An alert to the responsible clinician when risk rises

Who it’s for

Everyone signs in to the same place and sees only what their role needs.

Hospitals

See which of your patients are at risk, whether each one has a care team, and which follow-ups are still open.

Insurers

Follow adherence and its cost across your members, without opening anyone’s medical details unless there is a reason to.

Doctors and nurses

Start the day with your own patients: who is at risk, who is new to your care, and what needs your attention.

Case managers

Receive follow-up requests from the care team, contact the patient, and record what happened.

Patients

If your hospital uses Preventra, sign in with the account they gave you to see your own record. Only you and your care team can see it.

A reason before details

Approved accounts
