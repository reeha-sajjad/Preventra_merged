About Preventra

Preventra is a clinical analytics platform for care teams. One account gives access to two products: GLP-1 Analytics and Readmission Risk.

GLP-1 Analytics

Follows patients on GLP-1 therapy: how they respond to treatment, how well they keep to it, and what it returns for the payer.

Readmission Risk

  • Scores each patient's risk of returning to hospital within 30 days, at discharge.
  • Re-scores that risk every week across the post-discharge window as monitoring data arrives.
  • Explains what drives each score, so the care team knows what to act on.
  • Alerts the responsible clinician when a patient's risk rises, in a clinician console.

Who uses it

Doctors, nurses and case managers caring for discharged patients; hospital administrators; insurers; and patients, who see their own record only. New accounts are approved by an administrator before they can see any patient data.

Signing in

Everyone signs in once, on the Preventra sign-in page, and is then taken to the products their account has access to.
